OUR PROCESS

Good outcomes begin with a clear process.

Whether we are developing a physical structure or a digital system, our process begins by understanding the real problem before defining the solution.

DISCOVER

Understand

We begin with objectives, requirements, stakeholders, constraints and the context surrounding the project.

DEFINE

Define the path

We translate the requirement into scope, priorities, architecture, responsibilities and a practical delivery strategy.

DESIGN

Design with purpose

Concepts become coordinated designs, technical documentation, prototypes, calculations and decisions.

BUILD

Execute carefully

Engineering, construction and development move forward through disciplined execution, coordination and quality control.

EVOLVE

Deliver & improve

Delivery is followed by review, handover, support and improvement so the result can continue performing beyond launch.

The exact workflow changes with the project. A structural engineering assignment and a software platform require different technical methods, but both benefit from clear requirements, coordination, verification and accountable delivery.
